# Selection receipt — Canopy Relay

## Capability and distinctness

Canopy Relay is a camera-free urban-ravine field-operations product that ingests trusted sensor evidence, composes spatial, temporal, constrained-network, and local-neural mechanisms, policy-gates a reversible relay response, and durably retrieves its linked decision evidence.

It differs from recent incident recovery, patch verification, context provenance, supply continuity, contract drift, delegated authority, and model-routing products. Its operational object is a synthetic habitat state and its user outcome is a governed field intervention—not a generic dashboard, validator, or CLI.
